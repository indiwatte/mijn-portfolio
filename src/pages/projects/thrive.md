<details name="chapter" open>
<summary>The battle</summary>

Thrive's audience are everyday people trying to balance their health with a social life. They fight **small daily battles**, like choosing the healthy option, and that's what the boxing metaphor is about.

The campaign makes those choices feel good, and positions Thrive as the drink that helps you win them.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>Your new players</summary>

To bring Thrive Play and Thrive Unwind IPA to a broader audience, I introduced them as **"Your new players in the field"**: teammates in your daily health battles.

Play is for active, social moments; Unwind is for relaxing. That makes each drink's benefit clear and shows exactly when to reach for which one.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>Visual language</summary>

A minimal look wouldn't fit a brand that wants to be a game-changer, so I brought the battle to life visually: boxing rings, ropes, splashes and bold type.

The campaign got its own identity while staying true to Thrive. **Red** became the battle colour (aggressive and energetic), with **lime green** as accent and call-to-action colour. Thrive's typical blue tints and key elements stay, so the brand is instantly recognisable.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>Looping awareness</summary>

The campaign loops between the real world and the website:

1. **Find the arena:** QR codes and arrows on the ground guide people to the automated boxball machines.
2. **Fight the boxball:** a short, dynamic and accessible challenge that still feels rewarding.
3. **Scan your code:** a personal code on the screen takes you straight to the website.
4. **Get your battle card:** upload it in a simple, dedicated section. Its unique QR code proves it's real and earns you a free sample.
5. **Share it:** post your card directly on social media with #fightyourbattle. Each month, five winners get a free sample pack.

</details>
