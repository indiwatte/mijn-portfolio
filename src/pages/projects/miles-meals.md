<details name="chapter" open>
<summary>The idea</summary>

What if dinner could take you somewhere? Miles & Meals turns authentic street food and local stories into an at-home experience. Each box explores a city, neighbourhood or food culture through carefully selected recipes and ingredients.

No tourist traps. No generic meal kits. Just good food, real stories and a new place to discover.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>Visual language</summary>

I wanted Miles & Meals to feel **premium without becoming polished**, and authentic without becoming cliché.

The identity combines bold typography with handwritten accents, vibrant colours and tactile imagery. The visual language takes inspiration from street art, city architecture, mosaics and travel memories.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>The brand</summary>

One brand, three personalities. The identity stays recognisable while each box gets its own character: Street Made, Local Legend and Neighbourhood Bakes.

</details>

<details name="chapter">
<summary>The experience</summary>

The goal wasn't simply to design another food box. It was to create a small travel experience that happens at home — something you can open, cook, share and remember.

A box of ingredients. A whole world to discover.

</details>
